\ReportSection{The fix --- a data change, not an algorithm rewrite}
\vspace{-2pt}Index the competitor interchange numbers Caterpillar \emph{already publishes} so the search box resolves them --- the same way Cat\textquotesingle s own numbers already redirect to the product. A shopper holding a competitor number is the highest-intent buyer on the site, and today gets nothing.

\ReportSection{How this was measured, and what it covers}
\vspace{-2pt}{\fontsize{9}{11.4}\selectfont\color{NVIInk}\textbf{Measured, live.} 25 demand-weighted numbers, each run once on the shopper-facing search --- Cat 6/6 resolve, competitor 0/19; bot protection respected, no crawling or load; every cross-reference is Caterpillar\textquotesingle s own. \textbf{Scope.} A deliberate filter-category sample: filters are the canonical cross-reference purchase --- a mechanic reads the number off the old part --- and Caterpillar publishes an official filter cross-reference, so every mapping here is Cat\textquotesingle s own. The same indexing gap applies wherever a buyer arrives with a competitor number (batteries, ground-engaging tools); sizing the dollar opportunity needs Caterpillar\textquotesingle s own filter-category sales and traffic mix, which we have not assumed.}
