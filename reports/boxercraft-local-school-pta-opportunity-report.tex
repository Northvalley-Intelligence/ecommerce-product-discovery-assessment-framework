\Section{Attention Hook}
\ReportBody{For a PTA member about 20 miles away, the public site does not expose an elementary-school browse category. The observed school-specific browse nodes are Lambert-related, and those labels returned 0 search and 0 autocomplete products in the GEN-007 parity pass. The site can look like a general apparel catalog rather than the nearby answer for school shirts.}

\Section{Executive Diagnosis}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}Z{0.27\linewidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Likely PTA reaction} & \textbf{Why this matters}\\
\midrule
"I do not see my kind of school." & No observed elementary-school category; school browse signals are narrow and Lambert-related.\\
"Search did not help me." & School labels can have browse inventory while returning 0 search/autocomplete products.\\
"I need examples and ordering confidence." & PTA buyers need minimums, timeline, artwork help, sizes, approvals, reorders, and a person to contact.\\
"A local printer looks simpler." & The clipboard benchmark shows Boxercraft behind local competitors on Local Visibility, Message Clarity, Trust Signals, and AI Discoverability.\\

\Section{Priority Moves}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}Z{0.28\linewidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Move} & \textbf{Executive reason}\\
\midrule
Make schools a first-class buyer path & Add visible navigation and a landing path for elementary schools, PTAs, staff shirts, field day shirts, fundraisers, clubs, spiritwear, and reorders.\\
Align school terms across search, autocomplete, and browse & If a school browse node exists, the same label should work in search and autocomplete. This is the fastest bridge from the attention hook to the broader discovery problem.\\
Add local school proof & Show nearby school examples, group-order process, quote expectations, turnaround guidance, artwork help, and approved local service details.\\
Package the PTA job, not just the catalog & Volunteers need a low-risk path: choose apparel, upload or request artwork help, confirm sizes, approve, reorder, and contact a person.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
