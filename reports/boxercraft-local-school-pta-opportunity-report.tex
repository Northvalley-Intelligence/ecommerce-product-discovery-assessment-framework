\Section{How The Problem Surfaced}
\ReportBody{For a PTA member about 20 miles away, Boxercraft should feel like a natural local option for school T-shirts. Instead, the public site does not expose an elementary-school browse category. The high-level school signal visible in the captured browse evidence was narrow and Lambert-related, so the site immediately felt more like a general apparel catalog than a nearby school-shirt partner.}

\vspace{0.03in}
\ReportBody{That first miss led to the next check: if browse does not clearly show the school path, can search or autocomplete recover it? The answer was no for the observed school labels. School-specific browse pages existed, but the corresponding school labels did not return matching products through search or autocomplete in the captured parity pass.}

\Section{Problem Chain}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}Z{0.25\linewidth}Z{0.32\linewidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Buyer question} & \textbf{What the site makes visible} & \textbf{How it contributes to the PTA problem}\\
\midrule
Do you serve elementary schools? & No observed elementary-school browse path in the captured public navigation evidence. & The buyer can assume Boxercraft is not aimed at their school type before exploring products.\\
Can I find school shirts quickly? & School browsing signals are narrow, while the broader catalog remains product-first. & The local school job is hidden inside apparel navigation instead of being presented as a known buying situation.\\
Will search rescue me? & Observed school browse labels did not recover matching products in search or autocomplete. & A volunteer who searches after browsing sees another dead end and loses confidence.\\
Can I trust this for a group order? & The public journey does not quickly connect school examples, ordering confidence, artwork help, timeline, and local proof. & A local printer with clearer school-order language can feel safer even if Boxercraft is nearby.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\Section{Problems Found After The Initial Hook}

\textbf{Site problem} & \textbf{Contribution to the PTA not choosing Boxercraft}\\
\midrule
School is not a clear top-level buying path & A PTA buyer arrives with a school problem, but the site asks them to translate that problem into product categories and search terms.\\
Elementary school intent is not visible & The buyer does not see their own school context reflected, so the local advantage is weakened before product evaluation starts.\\
Browse and search are disconnected & When a browse page exists but the same label does not work in search or autocomplete, the buyer experiences the site as inconsistent rather than helpful.\\
School evidence appears narrow & Lambert-related browse signals do not communicate that Boxercraft is broadly prepared for elementary schools, PTAs, staff shirts, clubs, events, or reorders.\\
Local trust is not established early & The site does not quickly answer the volunteer's practical concerns: examples, artwork process, ordering confidence, timing, approvals, and who will help.\\

\Section{Diagnosis}
\DarkCard{Customer Pain Point}{The site does not fail because a single page is missing. It fails this buyer because the school need is not carried consistently across navigation, browse, search, autocomplete, and trust-building content. A nearby PTA member can leave before discovering whether Boxercraft is actually a good fit.}
